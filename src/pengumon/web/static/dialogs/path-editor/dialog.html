Edit the probe path

nothing changes until Apply

The hops pengumon probes every second, nearest first. Rename them, change their role, reorder, group or remove them, or add one by its address; Apply makes the new path live. A removed hop stops being probed and its history is kept, hidden.

    Add a hop

    An IPv4 or IPv6 address, or a host name (probed at its IPv4 or IPv6 address, as picked). Test it before you apply.

    Rediscover

    Traces the path to 1.1.1.1, 8.8.8.8 and 9.9.9.9 and proposes every hop with a role and the reasons for it, compared with the path above. Nothing is applied until you press Apply.

    Sends the public hop addresses the trace found (never a private address from your network), as DNS queries through your resolver, to Team Cymru's public lookup service, which answers with the network that operates each one.
