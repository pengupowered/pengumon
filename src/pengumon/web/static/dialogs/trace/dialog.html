discover the hops to a host
See every hop between this host and a destinationA trace sends probes with rising time-to-live and lists who answers, from your PC through the router and the modem to the destination. Use it to find the ISP hops worth monitoring; each one can be added as a target with one click. Usually 5 to 15 s, up to about 45 s when hops do not answer.
